Extended ECM for Government

OpenText™ Extended ECM for Government is a comprehensive digital file solution tailored for modern, digital government administration at all levels. It provides compliant content lifecycle management, facilitates efficient processes, and fosters effective collaboration. Extended ECM for Government empowers public sector organizations to seamlessly transition from paper-based to electronic record files and streamline their business operations. Key features include an inbox, a file plan based on records management, an embedded viewer experience, and dynamic workflow capabilities.

In this click-tour, we are looking at Gina Becker, an intake officer to manage an incoming service request, that leads to the creation of a new case. Sophia Wang processes the case and starts a dynamic workflow to get the validation permit validated and approved by various stakeholders.
